## Research Areas & Core Concepts

When referencing specific architectural subsystems of PramaanGrid, please consider citing the corresponding technical pillars:

- **Bi-Temporal Visual Language Model (VLM) Verification**: Dual-perspective anchor verification matching structural landmarks (e.g., concrete abutments, masonry pillars, storm drains) between baseline citizen complaint photos and contractor cleanup submissions.
- **Micro-Escrow Municipal Smart Contracts**: Automated contractual escrow locking and fraud penalty slashing for urban local body sanitation tenders.
- **Spatial Triangulation & ZK Geo-Audits**: Device-level telemetry cross-checked against municipal ward geographic boundaries and carrier tower triangulation to prevent GPS spoofing.
- **Statutory 12-Hour Citizen SLA Protocol**: Publicly verifiable timestamped audit trail enforcing urban sanitation resolution deadlines.
